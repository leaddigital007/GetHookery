## Problem (form-style, ~80 words)

> Performance marketing teams are stuck between two broken options.
> Traditional production (agencies, freelancers, UGC creators) costs
> $500–2,000 per ad and ships in 7–14 days; 90% of creatives don't
> perform. Generic AI tools (Runway, Pika, Veo) deliver beautiful
> generation with zero brand control — characters change between
> shots and product packaging looks different every time. Performance
> teams test 50–100 creatives per week. They need volume **and**
> brand consistency. No tool delivers both.

## Solution (form-style, ~80 words)

> One platform, four studios, multi-model backend. Reference Studio
> generates images from up to 5 reference photos for consistent
> character / product shots. Marketing Studio produces UGC-style ad
> video with avatar + product + dialogue. Storyframes turns prompts
> into cinematic storyboards. Kubricon Cinema strings multi-shot
> scenes with reusable characters and locations. Backed by Veo 3.1,
> Kling 3.0, Seedance 2.0, Flux 2 Max, GPT Image 2 — best model per
> shot, no vendor lock-in.

## Why now (form-style, ~80 words)

> Three curves crossed in the last 18 months. (1) AI video became
> production-usable: Veo 3.1 native audio + reference-image control
> at $0.05/sec 720p. (2) Performance marketing demand exploded: US
> digital video ad revenue $78B in 2025 (+25% YoY); influencer
> marketing $12.4B in 2026, with micro/nano creators at 45.5% of
> spend (up from 19.5% five years ago). (3) Marketers are ready: 27%
> name creative production as the area most disrupted by AI; 75%
> already report AI has increased content volume.

## Differentiation (form-style, ~50 words)

> The only platform combining multi-model AI generation with
> director-level control (reference characters, products, brand kits)
> in studios purpose-built for performance marketing jobs. Built by a
> performance marketer for performance marketers — not by AI
> researchers building for "creators" in the abstract.

## Defensibility (form-style, ~80 words)

> Three things compound. (1) Workflow depth — a single-shot generator
> is replaced when a better model ships, but a studio workflow with
> reference libraries, brand kits and shot lists is replaced only when
> a customer rebuilds their entire production process. (2) Founder-
> market fit — performance marketing requires real industry knowledge
> generic AI labs lack. (3) Performance data flywheel — once usage
> scales, generation outcomes tied to ad performance create a feedback
> loop competitors can't replicate without our customer data.
